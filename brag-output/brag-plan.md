# VIS Legal Abogados — Brag Video Plan

**Format:** Vertical (9:16)  
**Tone:** Polished  
**Duration:** 18 seconds  
**Platform:** Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts

---

## The 9-Question Rubric

1. **What is the app?**  
   VIS Legal Abogados is an Ecuador-based law firm that combines rigorous legal expertise with a modern client portal, making legal services transparent, organized, and less mysterious.

2. **What is the funniest/most impressive claim?**  
   _"Decisiones legales importantes merecen una estrategia comprensible"_ (Important legal decisions deserve a clear strategy) — the insight that legal services can be understandable and human-centered, not cryptic.

3. **What is the visual hook?**  
   The premium visual identity: deep forest green (#173d36) + warm gold accents (#c99a5b) + ivory backgrounds. The sophisticated case-card UI in the portal is the strongest asset — a real client seeing their case progress, documents, and team communication in one place.

4. **What should be shown from actual UI?**  
   - Hero section with the tagline "Tu defensa empieza con claridad"
   - The Portal Card mockup showing case status (68%), document count, last update
   - The three-step method: Entendemos → Diseñamos → Acompañamos
   - The WhatsApp CTA button ("Solicitar consulta")

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**  
   18 seconds: hook (mission/tagline) → reveal portal in use (case progress, documents) → method/values → contact CTA.

6. **What tone fits best?**  
   **Preset:** Polished  
   **Creative direction:** "Quiet premium product film for a legal-tech platform — composed, confident, no hype."

7. **What should the audio feel like?**  
   Warm, sophisticated audio bed. Minor-key piano or strings with subtle ambient texture. No narration. Motion-matched SFX for UI reveals: soft clicks for card/button interactions, a gentle pulse for progress bars. Fade to silence 1s before final CTA frame.

8. **What should the share caption say?**  
   "Legal services that make sense. Your case, organized. Your team, visible. Your next step, clear. VIS Legal Abogados. 🔒"

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**  
   Contact form → Portal access → Live case tracking (documents updated, progress visible, team nearby) → Communication. The video should feel like a user opening the portal for the first time and seeing their case come into focus.

## Visual Identity Notes

- **Primary color:** Forest green (#173d36) — used for hero bg, cards, footer  
- **Accent color:** Gold (#c99a5b) — headlines, button highlights, accents  
- **Neutral:** Ivory (#f7f4ee) — text on dark, card/surfaces on dark  
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (serif, headlines); System stack (sans, body)  
- **UI kit:** iOS-inspired cards, minimal borders, 1.2rem radius, subtle shadows  
- **Animation:** Apple easing (cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)), 0.3–0.5s transitions

---

## Audio Cue Guidance

**Music bed:** Warm, minor-key piano or strings loop (18s, ~90–100 BPM). No vocals. Ambient texture underneath (pad synth, subtle reverb). Fade out at 16s over 1s.

**SFX:** 
- Card reveal: soft UI click (0–2dB above bed)
- Progress bar pulse: gentle tone sync (50ms hits at 0.5s intervals)
- Document/file: subtle flutter or page-turn sound
- Method cards: staggered UI reveals (3× light clicks)
- Message notification: soft ping (short, 100ms)
- Button press: deeper click, haptic-like (120–150Hz)

**Silence:** Last 1s is near-silence (bed fully faded, just ambient tail).
